## Description (draft)

ListingLens QC checks your product photos for the issues that most often hurt online
listings — low resolution, blur, bad exposure, poor framing, and accidental duplicates
— entirely on your device. Select up to 20 photos, get a plain-language quality score
for each, see a recommended posting order, and find your best "hero" photo, all without
your photos ever leaving your phone.

- No accounts, no ads, no tracking.
- Works fully offline.
- Deterministic, explainable scoring — every warning tells you what it measured.
